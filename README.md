 AKAN-NAME-GENERATOR
A web application that calculates the day of the week a user was born and assigns them an Akan name based on their gender, following Ghanaian cultural traditions.

🌍 Project Description
The Akan Name Generator is a simple web application that helps users discover their Akan name based on their birth date and gender. In Ghanaian culture, particularly among the Akan people of Ghana, Ivory Coast, and Togo, children are traditionally named according to the day of the week they were born. This practice reflects the deep cultural significance of birth order and timing in African traditions.

📚 Background
The Akan people believe that the day a person is born influences their character and destiny. Each day of the week has corresponding names for both males and females. For example:

A male born on Sunday is named Kwasi

A female born on Sunday is named Akosua

This naming tradition is still widely practiced in Ghana and among the diaspora communities worldwide.
